Scope and changes

This list covers the infrastructure, sign-in, and billing providers identified in the current Privacy Policy. Support correspondence may use communications providers; a specific support provider is not identified in that policy. Contact us for that information before sending data with contractual processing requirements.

External services you connect from a workload receive the data you send them and are your chosen destinations. This list does not enumerate them or each provider’s own subprocessors.

We will update this page and its review date when the listed services change. No contractual subprocessor-change notification or objection process is offered by this page. See DPA availability and contact support@mainbrella.com for processing questions.
