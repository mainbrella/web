Before you start

Can I replace the E2B SDK without changing my code?

No. Mainbrella has its own JavaScript and Python SDKs and HTTP API. Adapt your creation, command, file, and cleanup calls; start with one representative workload.

What happens when I reach a limit?

New starts are blocked when your start or compute allowance is exhausted. Machine deadlines enforce reserved runtime. Builder sessions last up to one hour, with a 10-minute idle timeout. See the full limits before choosing a plan.

Does a saved workspace resume a running agent?

No. Saved workspaces preserve filesystem snapshots and restore into a fresh container. They do not preserve running processes or conversation state. Export or save your files before stopping.

Is Mainbrella open source?

Mainbrella publishes its platform source for inspection and self-hosting. Hosted accounts use Mainbrella’s managed service. See the repositories and license details.
