Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how Mainbrella’s developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles personal information for mainbrella.com, website accounts, subscription billing, and any sandbox access we provide. We are responsible for the account, billing, and support information we use to operate Mainbrella.

Current service status

Mainbrella is developing a Cloudflare-powered sandbox service for AI agents. Public sandbox access is not yet available. The website supports Google sign-in and Stripe-hosted Pro subscription checkout. The sections below distinguish that current flow from information processed if sandbox access is provided.

Account and sign-in information

When you sign in with Google, we receive and verify a Google identity credential and store your Google account identifier, email address, name, and a Mainbrella user identifier. We also store account creation and session records so we can authenticate requests and associate your subscription with your account. This sign-in flow does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, or Google password.

Subscription and payment information

We send your name, email address, and Mainbrella user identifier to Stripe to create a billing customer and associate checkout with your account. We store Stripe customer and checkout identifiers and retrieve subscription status, billing-period information, and cancellation status to display and manage your subscription.

You enter payment details on Stripe-hosted checkout and billing pages. Our website and subscription API do not receive or store your full card number or card security code. Stripe handles payment, billing, and fraud-prevention information under its Privacy Policy. The current subscription flow does not meter sandbox compute usage.

Website requests and cookies

Cloudflare hosts the website and API and stores account and billing records in its database infrastructure. It may process IP addresses, browser and device information, request paths and times, and traffic or security logs to route requests, deliver the Services, diagnose problems, and prevent abuse.

Our API sets a secure, HTTP-only cookie named mainbrella_session after sign-in. It lasts up to 30 days unless you sign out or the session is revoked. A hashed session token and expiration record are stored on the server. The cookie is used for authentication, not advertising. Signing out revokes the current session and expires its cookie; it does not delete your account or cancel billing.

The website does not currently include advertising or independent analytics scripts. Google’s sign-in script loads when you choose to sign in to subscribe. Google and Stripe may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you interact with their services. Browser settings can block or delete cookies, but blocking the session cookie may prevent sign-in and subscription management.

Sandbox content, if access is provided

A cloud sandbox processes data on remote infrastructure. Code, commands, files, prompts included in workloads, environment variables, and outputs you submit may be processed by Mainbrella and Cloudflare to run your workload. Workspace files and snapshots may be stored where those features are provided, and operational records may contain resource identifiers, execution times, errors, or usage information. Do not assume that sandbox content stays on your device.

You control what you place in a sandbox and which external services your code contacts. Those destinations receive information sent by your workload under their own policies. Limit credentials and personal information to what a task requires. Sandbox isolation does not prevent your own code from accessing or transmitting information you give it.

If we process personal data in a workload on your organization’s behalf, that processing must follow your lawful instructions and any applicable written data processing agreement. Contact us before submitting data that requires such an agreement or special safeguards. We do not claim that Daytona’s data processing agreement covers Mainbrella.

Support and purposes of use

If you contact us, we receive your contact details, message, and any attachments you choose to send. Please avoid sending passwords, secret keys, or unnecessary sensitive information.

We use information to authenticate accounts, provide and manage subscriptions, operate and secure the Services, respond to support requests, resolve billing issues, communicate service or policy changes, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on performing our agreement with you for account and billing services, legitimate interests in maintaining security and responding to requests, legal obligations for required records, and consent where required. You may withdraw consent for processing based on consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Providers and disclosure

Our current providers include Cloudflare for hosting, API execution, and database infrastructure; Google for sign-in; and Stripe for checkout, recurring payments, and the billing portal. Cloudflare also provides the infrastructure for Mainbrella’s sandbox development. We may use communications providers to handle support.

We share information as needed to deliver those services, comply with legal requirements, or protect rights and address fraud or abuse. Providers may also process information for their own purposes as described in their policies. Daytona’s policies were reviewed as a reference for sandbox services; Daytona is not part of the current website sign-in or subscription flow.

Retention, security, and international processing

Account, billing, and support records are kept for as long as needed to provide the Services, address disputes, and meet legal recordkeeping obligations. Session validity is limited to 30 days; expired session records are cleaned up when new sessions are created. An expiration date is not a promise that all account or provider records are deleted on that date.

We have not published a guaranteed sandbox or snapshot retention period. If sandbox access is provided, do not treat it as permanent storage; keep your own backups and ask about retention before relying on it. Deletion requests may be subject to required billing records, security needs, and provider backup retention.

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own. We do not currently promise a particular data residency region. Where required by law, international transfers must have appropriate safeguards. Contact us about any residency or transfer requirements before submitting personal data in workloads.

We use HTTPS, secure session cookies, and hashed session tokens. These measures reduce risk but do not guarantee that transmission, storage, or sandbox execution is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You can browse without signing in, sign out of an active session, and manage or cancel a subscription through “Manage subscription” in the pricing section. Contact support@mainbrella.com to request account deletion. Cancel recurring billing separately; deleting or blocking cookies does not cancel a subscription.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a portable copy of personal information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law permits or requires it. For data submitted on behalf of an organization, contact that organization first so it can direct the request.

Children, changes, and contact

The Services are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

We will update this policy and its date as our practices change, including before introducing materially different sandbox data processing. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@mainbrella.com.
