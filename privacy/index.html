Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how Andrew Arrow, doing business as Mainbrella Co., a sole proprietorship in Culver City, California, United States (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles personal information for mainbrella.com, website accounts, subscription billing, and any sandbox access we provide. We are responsible for the account, billing, and support information we use to operate Mainbrella.

Current service status

Mainbrella provides Cloudflare-powered Linux containers for AI agents to signed-in users with active Builder, Pro, or Scale subscriptions, or valid coupon trials. The website supports account sign-in, Stripe embedded checkout, and subscription management.

Account and sign-in information

When you sign in with Google, we receive and verify a Google identity credential and store your Google account identifier, email address, name, and a Mainbrella user identifier. We also store account creation and session records so we can authenticate requests and associate your subscription with your account. This sign-in flow does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, or Google password.

API keys and access records

If you create an API key, we store its name, public prefix, credential hash, creation time, last-use time, and account association. The complete secret is shown only when created. Keys stay valid until revoked, independently of browser sign-out. SSH access tokens are stored as hashes with a container generation and expiration time.

Subscription and payment information

New subscriptions require sign-in. Existing guest purchases are not automatically linked to a Mainbrella account. For signed-in purchases, we send your name, email address, and Mainbrella user identifier to Stripe to create a billing customer and associate checkout with your account. We store Stripe customer and checkout identifiers and retrieve subscription status, billing-period information, and cancellation status to display and manage your subscription.

You enter payment details in Stripe’s embedded payment form on our website and Stripe-hosted billing pages. Our website and subscription API do not receive or store your full card number or card security code. Stripe handles payment, billing, and fraud-prevention information under its Privacy Policy. The current subscription flow does not meter sandbox compute usage.

Website requests and cookies

Cloudflare hosts the website and API and stores account and billing records in its database infrastructure. It may process IP addresses, browser and device information, request paths and times, and traffic or security logs to route requests, deliver the Services, diagnose problems, and prevent abuse.

Our API sets a secure, HTTP-only cookie named mainbrella_session after sign-in. It lasts up to 30 days unless you sign out or the session is revoked. A hashed session token and expiration record are stored on the server. The cookie is used for authentication, not advertising. Signing out revokes the current session and expires its cookie; it does not delete your account or cancel billing.

The homepage asks for your cookie preference on your first visit. Choosing “Accept All” enables the Google tag, OpenAI measurement pixel, and Taboola page-view pixel; choosing “Reject All” keeps these optional scripts from loading. Your choice is saved in your browser so you are not asked on each visit. Clearing this website’s browser storage resets the choice. After “Reject All,” sign-in and account features show a message explaining that they need cookies. Select “Change cookie choice” to reopen the same “Reject All” / “Accept All” dialog. Choosing “Accept All” enables sign-in and optional tracking. Google’s sign-in script loads only after cookies are accepted. Google and Stripe may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you interact with their services. Browser settings can block or delete cookies, but blocking the session cookie may prevent sign-in and subscription management.

Container content

A cloud sandbox processes data on remote infrastructure. Code, commands, files, prompts included in workloads, environment variables, and outputs you submit may be processed by Mainbrella and Cloudflare to run your workload. Workspace files are temporary, and operational records may contain resource identifiers, execution times, errors, or usage information. Custom image builds also process the Dockerfile and uploaded build context, and retain owned image records and build logs so you can inspect and use the image. Do not assume that sandbox content stays on your device.

You control what you place in a sandbox and which external services your code contacts. Those destinations receive information sent by your workload under their own policies. Limit credentials and personal information to what a task requires. Sandbox isolation does not prevent your own code from accessing or transmitting information you give it.

If we process personal data in a workload on your organization’s behalf, that processing must follow your lawful instructions and any applicable written data processing agreement. Contact us before submitting data that requires such an agreement or special safeguards.

Support and purposes of use

If you contact us, we receive your contact details, message, and any attachments you choose to send. Please avoid sending passwords, secret keys, or unnecessary sensitive information.

We use information to authenticate accounts, provide and manage subscriptions, operate and secure the Services, respond to support requests, resolve billing issues, communicate service or policy changes, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on performing our agreement with you for account and billing services, legitimate interests in maintaining security and responding to requests, legal obligations for required records, and consent where required. You may withdraw consent for processing based on consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Providers and disclosure

See the provider and subprocessor list for service purposes, data categories, and processing locations, and the Trust center for current DPA availability.

Our current providers include Cloudflare for hosting, API execution, and database infrastructure; Google for sign-in; and Stripe for checkout, recurring payments, and the billing portal. Cloudflare also provides the infrastructure for Mainbrella’s containers. We may use communications providers to handle support.

We share information as needed to deliver those services, comply with legal requirements, or protect rights and address fraud or abuse. Providers may also process information for their own purposes as described in their policies.

Retention, security, and international processing

Account, billing, and support records are kept for as long as needed to provide the Services, address disputes, and meet legal recordkeeping obligations. Session validity is limited to 30 days; expired session records are cleaned up when new sessions are created. An expiration date is not a promise that all account or provider records are deleted on that date.

Containers are temporary. Explicitly saved filesystem snapshots have plan-specific retention; ordinary stop discards unsaved files. Keep your own backups. Deletion requests may be subject to required billing records, security needs, and provider backup retention.

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own. We do not currently promise a particular data residency region. Where required by law, international transfers must have appropriate safeguards. Contact us about any residency or transfer requirements before submitting personal data in workloads.

We use HTTPS, secure session cookies, and hashed session tokens and API keys. These measures reduce risk but do not guarantee that transmission, storage, or sandbox execution is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You can browse without signing in, sign out of an active session, update payment details through “Manage subscription,” and cancel an account-linked subscription through “Cancel subscription” in the pricing section. Contact support@mainbrella.com for guest subscription management or to request account deletion. Cancel recurring billing separately; deleting or blocking cookies does not cancel a subscription.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a portable copy of personal information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law permits or requires it. For data submitted on behalf of an organization, contact that organization first so it can direct the request.

Children, changes, and contact

The Services are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

We will update this policy and its date as our practices change, including before introducing materially different sandbox data processing. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
