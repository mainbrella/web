Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how mainbrella’s developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use mainbrella on iOS or Android and visit mainbrella.com.

Photos and drawings

mainbrella processes photos and creates artwork on your device. We do not upload your photos or drawings to our servers or receive copies of them. Photo conversion does not require an account or an internet connection.

You choose which photo to open using your device’s photo picker, or allow camera access to take a photo. You can manage the app’s permissions in your iOS or Android device settings. If you save or share artwork, the destination you choose handles that copy under its own privacy practices.

App information

The app does not require registration and does not include advertising or third-party analytics trackers. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Apple and Google may process download, purchase, and diagnostic information through the App Store, Google Play, and their operating systems under their own policies and your account or device settings. They may make app diagnostic reports available to us through their developer services. Those reports do not give us access to your photo library or drawings.

Website information

Our website does not use analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or set its own cookies. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, requested pages, and request times to deliver the website, maintain security, and prevent abuse.

Technical information is used to operate and protect the website. Hosting providers may process it in countries other than your own, under their applicable privacy practices and retention policies.

Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your contact details and message. We use it to answer your request and resolve issues, and keep it only as long as needed for those purposes or to meet legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive information or photos you do not want us to receive.

If you join the Android tester group, Google handles your participation through Google Groups. Your name, email address, and any messages you post may be visible to group owners, managers, or other members, depending on the group’s settings. We use information you share with us to manage tester access and respond to feedback. You can manage your membership and email preferences through Google Groups.

Sharing and security

Information may be handled by service providers supporting website hosting or communications, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights and prevent abuse. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You control photo selection, camera permissions, and where you save or share artwork. Removing the app does not remove images you have saved elsewhere. We cannot access or delete photos and drawings on your device.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to or restrict its use. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support message, contact us so we can address it.

Other services

The App Store, Google Play, Google Groups, and other services have their own privacy policies. You can read Apple’s Privacy Policy, Google’s Privacy Policy, and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for more information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our practices change. We will post the updated policy here and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before making material changes.

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@mainbrella.com.
