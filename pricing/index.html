What does a compute-unit hour buy?

One compute-unit hour runs a Lite machine for one hour. Larger sizes use more units. Running and provisioning time, including idle time, is metered. Stopped machines incur no compute charges.

Before you subscribe

How does the spending cap work?

Choose a cap from $5 to $1,000 per billing month, before taxes. The default is $5, so no overages are possible until you raise the cap. Starting a sandbox reserves its maximum runtime against the cap; stopping releases unused runtime. Only elapsed runtime is billed. New sessions may be shortened or blocked when the cap is committed. Usage alerts appear in billing at 50%, 80%, and 100%. Monthly start limits reset on the first of each UTC month.

Can I cancel or keep my existing plan?

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your paid period; outstanding usage is still payable. Existing Builder, Pro, and Scale subscriptions keep their prices and allowances. You can switch to usage billing at renewal from your billing controls. See the Terms of Service.

Do files persist when a workspace stops?

Save a workspace before stopping to restore its filesystem into a fresh container. Ordinary stop discards unsaved changes. Saved workspaces have plan-specific quotas and retention. Keep independent backups; see saved workspaces and file transfer.

Is there an SLA or priority support?

Self-service plans include no uptime SLA, guaranteed support response time, or automatic service credits. Review the trust center and service status, or contact Andrew about your requirements before subscribing.
