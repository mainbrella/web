Plan rules and included access

All plans use the same container: 1/16 vCPU, 256 MiB RAM and 2 GB ephemeral disk, with Node 24, bash, tmux, outbound internet, SSH and browser terminals. Each container allows four terminal connections; each account allows ten live SSH commands, valid for up to 15 minutes.

Starts are shared across your account and reset at the start of each UTC month. Failed starts count. Stopping a container or changing plans does not reset usage. Sessions end at their hard limit or the end of the paid period at creation, whichever comes first. Files are lost when a container stops.

Upgrades require payment through Stripe. Downgrades and cancellation take effect at renewal; lower limits may stop containers. Snapshots, larger machines, team seats, SDKs, advanced logs, audit exports and priority capacity are unavailable on every plan.

By subscribing, you authorize recurring monthly charges until you cancel and agree to the Terms of Service.
