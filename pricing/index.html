Monthly spending cap

Limits compute used each UTC month, from $5 to $1,000. Raising the cap does not add funds. Compute stops when funded runtime or the cap runs out.

Automatic recharge

By saving with recharge enabled, you authorize future charges to your saved card for the recharge amount when funding runs low, up to your recharge maximum each UTC month. You can disable recharge at any time. The compute spending cap applies separately.

Billing questions

Does my balance expire each month?

No. Unused prepaid funds carry forward. There is no monthly subscription or charge unless you explicitly enable automatic recharge.

How do reservations and the spending cap work?

Your current balance includes funding reserved for running machines. Available funding excludes those reservations. Only elapsed runtime is charged; stopping releases unused funds. Your monthly compute cap is an additional limit, independent of your balance and automatic recharge maximum.

Do files persist when a workspace stops?

Save a workspace before stopping to restore its filesystem into a fresh container. Ordinary stop discards unsaved changes. Keep independent backups; see saved workspaces and file transfer.

Is there an SLA or priority support?

Self-service compute includes no uptime SLA, guaranteed support response time, or automatic service credits. Review the trust center and service status, or contact Andrew about your requirements.
