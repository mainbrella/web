A little help. Or all you.

Four ways to draw the same picture.
Choose the level that feels right.

Magic
Pick a pen and move through its highlighted areas. Watch the picture fill with color.
Magnet
Follow the orange guide. Your marker snaps to the line as you move.
Trace
Draw over a faint guide. Every mark is yours, with a little direction underneath.
Solo
Keep a small reference picture nearby and draw freely. Your lines, your way.

Your photos stay on your device. Photo conversion works on the device, without an account or an internet connection.
