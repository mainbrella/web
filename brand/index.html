Description

Mainbrella provides temporary Linux workspaces for AI agents, with an HTTP API, SSH, and browser terminal access.

Logo and wordmarks

The umbrella mark is the existing 256 × 256 transparent PNG. SVG files embed that original raster mark; they are not fully vector artwork. Wordmark PNGs are 1040 × 256 with transparent backgrounds.

Usage

  • Write the product name as Mainbrella in prose; keep the wordmark lowercase.
  • Keep the original colors and proportions. Leave clear space of at least one quarter of the mark’s width on all sides.
  • Use the appropriate wordmark for the background. Keep the umbrella at least 32 px wide and the full wordmark at least 160 px wide.
  • Avoid cropping, stretching, recoloring, adding effects, or placing the logo on a busy background.
  • Use the assets to identify Mainbrella accurately. Do not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or a partnership.
