## Prepaid billing

New accounts purchase $5–$1,000 of prepaid compute balance through `/billing/topups`. There is no monthly subscription or monthly minimum charge. Unused funding carries forward. Stripe promotion codes backed by Stripe coupons may reduce the price without reducing the selected compute balance; without a discount, each dollar paid adds one dollar of balance. For example, a 50% code on a $20 top-up charges $10 and adds $20 of compute balance. All prepaid accounts use the same prices and limits. Client-supplied credit amounts are ignored. The Mainbrella trial-coupon system is separate. Automatic recharges do not apply promotion codes. Compute costs $0.02 per compute-unit hour; weighted hourly rates are Lite $0.02, Small $0.12, Medium $0.20, Large $0.32 and XL $0.56. Provisioning and idle time count; only elapsed allocation is billed. Ephemeral disk is included. Storage, dedicated IPs and email have no separately enabled usage charges yet.

Mainbrella keeps the wallet in the durable account controller. Available funding is confirmed payment credit minus elapsed consumption minus reserved future runtime. Reservations happen before provisioning and unused reservations are released after confirmed stops. All clients must honor returned `expiresAt`; Ad Hoc machines retain the 24-hour session and 30-minute idle deadlines. Production renews funded five-minute reservations and requires at least 24 hours of fleet funding at launch. The monthly consumption cap remains separate from prepaid balance; raising it never creates funding.

Browser-only `GET /billing/balance` returns balance, available and reserved USD cents, monthly usage/cap, production hourly cost and funded runway, and automatic recharge status. Explicit `/billing/settings` consent enables automatic recharges with a fixed amount and monthly maximum. The durable pending attempt is saved before contacting Stripe. Metadata and retained payment IDs recover unknown outcomes after HTTP idempotency keys expire; an old unknown attempt fails closed. Succeeded captured card payments fund runtime; pending, failed or authentication-required payments do not.

Browser-only `GET /billing/history` returns `{asOf,balance,totals,currentHourlyCents,activeResources,resources,fundings,nextResourceCursor,nextFundingCursor,historyTruncated,retainedResourceLimit}`. `asOf` and timestamps are epoch milliseconds. Lifetime `totals` contains `fundedCents` (original purchased credit), `revokedCents` (current refund/dispute deductions), `usedCents` (elapsed compute), and `unattributedUsedCents` (consumption whose resource detail is unavailable). The balance equals the floor of funding minus deductions minus consumption; reservations reduce available funds separately. Consumption and reservations use fractional USD cents without per-allocation rounding.

Each resource has `{id,containerId,name,lifecycle,size,startAt,endAt,runtimeMs,computeUnitHours,usedCents,reservedCents,hourlyCents,active}`. UUID `id` identifies one allocation even when a container slot is reused; production lease renewals retain the allocation identity. `name` can be null. `startAt` begins attributable runtime and may be later than the original allocation start for machines predating tracking. Current retained leases are always in `activeResources`, independent of pagination; expired leases awaiting confirmed stop have `active:false` and `endAt:null`. Only unexpired leases contribute to `currentHourlyCents`. Completed resources have a settled `endAt` and no reservation. Startup, idle and executing time all count as allocated compute; commands, requests, storage, dedicated IPs and email have no additional prepaid charge.

`resources` retains the newest 256 completed allocations plus all current leases. Earlier settled consumption and compacted records stay exact in `unattributedUsedCents`; `historyTruncated` signals unavailable resource attribution. `fundings` rows contain `{id,createdAt,amountCents,revokedCents,reason}`; reason is null, `refund` or `dispute`. `createdAt` is purchase time, and deductions reflect current state, not an invented reversal event/date. Both completed-resource and funding pages are newest-first; `limit` defaults to 50 and accepts 1–100. Supply the respective returned cursor to continue each list independently. Lifetime totals remain complete regardless of pagination. An unknown or compacted cursor returns 400 `invalid_history_cursor`; restart that list from its first page. Pages reflect fresh snapshots rather than a frozen multi-request transaction. Reads never mutate billing, reconcile Stripe, renew a lease or provision a guest, and remain available without Stripe configuration. Legacy subscription invoices are not included.

Checkout completion and signed Stripe webhooks verify the live Checkout subtotal, Stripe discount, customer and saved purchase identity. Nonzero totals additionally require current PaymentIntent and captured card charge state matching the actual amount paid, currency, customer, account metadata and funding purpose. A 100% discount requires a completed `no_payment_required` Checkout and uses its session ID as the funding identity. Funding is applied once per identity, across retries and event types. Refunds remove the corresponding proportion of compute credit, rounded up to the next cent; a dispute revokes the payment's entire balance contribution. A reversal can leave a negative wallet when credit was already consumed. Delayed success events cannot restore revoked funding. Dispute restoration requires operator reconciliation in this initial implementation.

Set `STRIPE_PREPAID_PRICE_ID` to an active one-time USD $5 Price with an active Product. The backend validates it; exact $5 purchases use that Price and other amounts use inline prices under its Product. Configure matching test or live `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`; new prepaid purchases require both, while completion verification and billing settings remain available without the publishable key. Apply migration `019_prepaid_billing.sql` and configure the existing signed `/subscription/webhook` for Checkout, PaymentIntent, charge/refund and dispute events before deployment. Existing legacy read, payment-method management, invoice reconciliation and cancellation remain available; new recurring purchases are disabled. See `docs/prepaid-billing.md` and `docs/usage-billing.md` for operator setup.
